\section{Purpose and certification boundary}
This supplement accompanies the final-audit revision of \emph{A Lean-certified proof of the Twin Prime Conjecture}.  The term \emph{Lean-certified} is used at an explicit publication boundary, not to mean that all cited analytic number theory has been re-proved from foundations in Lean.

The project distinguishes three layers:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=*]
\item external analytic theorems and quotient reductions, which remain mathematical inputs;
\item reproducible directed numerical certificates used for the lower coefficient; and
\item Lean-checked finite deductions, range arithmetic, theorem adapters and logical closure from the declared source statements.
\end{enumerate}
A source statement supplied through a Lean structure remains an input.  A clean axiom report certifies the formal implication from that input; it does not promote the input itself into a Lean proof of the underlying analytic theorem.

\section{The dependency-clean proof architecture}
The submitted mathematical argument uses one common weighted scale and an integrated coefficient crossing.  It does not attempt to make one strongest estimate hold uniformly on every critical cell.

On the lower side, the exact-$P_3$ region is divided according to the distribution mechanism actually available on each cell, and the more expensive sieve constant is paid only on the remaining small region.  The directed $P_3$ and $P_4$ numerical certificates then give the conservative lower coefficient
\[
 c_A>0.7541.
\]
On the upper side, the natural balanced-semiprime coefficient is required only on the fixed interior $t\le0.498$ and the terminal strip is paid at relative constant $8$.  The strip has mass fraction
\[
 \rho=\frac{\log(251/249)}{\log2}=0.0115416\ldots,
\]
so the integrated multiplier is
\[
 1+7\rho=1.0807913\ldots<\frac{0.7541}{\log2}=1.0879\ldots.
\]
The final manuscript therefore needs neither a uniform endpoint theorem nor any retired lower-side analytic branch.

\section{Archive policy}
Superseded exploratory branches, failed-and-repaired Lean sources, the old manually defined zero ledger, the earlier all-integration harness and chronological source records are intentionally excluded from the submission package.  They remain preserved separately in the project archive in \code{/TwinPrime/}.
